Invasive Environmental Threats

Non-native predatory fish introduced into Sri Lanka's waterways disrupt delicate aquatic ecosystems, outcompete indigenous species, and pose critical threats to endemic biodiversity.

Invasive Predator
English Name Alligator Gar
Scientific Name Atractosteus spatula
Sinhala Name ඇලිගේටර් ගාර්

A massive freshwater apex predator native to North America. Released into local ecosystems through the aquarium trade, its heavy armored scales and sharp, dual-row teeth make it a severe threat to local native fish populations.

High Ecological Risk
English Name Redline Snakehead
Scientific Name Channa micropeltes
Sinhala Name රතු ඉරි ආරා

Known as one of the largest and most aggressive species of snakeheads. Possessing extremely sharp teeth and parental protection behavior, it aggressively outcompetes native predator fish like Channa ara (සූඩොලූලා).

Invasive Carnivore
English Name Clown Knifefish
Scientific Name Chitala ornata
Sinhala Name මන්නා

A nocturnal carnivorous species originating from Southeast Asia. Widely established in rivers such as the Kelani, it feeds heavily on smaller native cyprinids and crustaceans, significantly reducing local aquatic biodiversity.
